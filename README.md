# Marketing-Campaign-Customer-Analysis-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze marketing campaign performance, customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and sales patterns, with a focus on understanding campaign outcomes and factors influencing customer purchases.

**Dashboard Views**

**1. Campaign Performance**

Campaign-wise purchase and sales comparisons

Product category performance across campaigns

Revenue contribution by product category

Purchase channel analysis across campaigns


**2. Buyer Composition**


Customer demographics including age, income, education, and marital status

Customer purchasing behavior across different channels

Product preferences across age groups

Customer purchase and engagement metrics


**3. Purchase Drivers**

Analysis of factors influencing campaign acceptance

Identification of customer characteristics associated with campaign responses

Customer segmentation based on purchasing patterns

Key influencers and segment-level sales analysis


**Tools Used**

Microsoft Power BI

Data Visualization

Interactive Dashboard Design

Key Influencers Analysis

Customer Segmentation
